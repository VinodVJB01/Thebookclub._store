About The Book Club Store

A Student-Led Initiative for a Reading Revival

The Book Club Store is a student-led initiative dedicated to nurturing reading habits among Indian readers and helping people reclaim their time from endless scrolling.

We believe reading is an antidote to many of the problems of modern 21st-century life. In a world where people spend hours glued to their screens, we are fighting what we playfully call the “zombie pandemic”—the habit of endlessly scrolling and becoming disconnected from the world around us.

Our mission is to encourage people to put their screens aside, rediscover the joy of books, develop curiosity, and make reading a meaningful part of everyday life.

Original Books. A Better Reading Experience.

We sell original books sourced directly from publishers. We believe books should be authentic, fairly represented, and enjoyable to read. Our prices are set at realistic levels rather than relying on suspiciously cheap, unauthentic copies.

We do not sell pirated books.

Book piracy is unfair to both authors and readers. Authors lose the royalties they deserve for their creative work, while readers may receive a poor-quality product that diminishes the reading experience.

Pirated copies may contain tiny or inconsistent fonts, low-quality ink, inferior paper, missing paragraphs, or even missing pages. These problems can turn reading into a frustrating experience instead of an enjoyable one.

We believe authors deserve recognition for their work, and readers deserve books they can genuinely enjoy. That is why we focus on supplying original paperbacks and hardcovers sourced from publishers.

Authenticity matters. A good book deserves a good reading experience.

Books Delivered to Your Door

We work to bring original books closer to readers through courier companies and India Post.

Whether you enjoy the feel of a paperback or prefer the durability of a hardcover, our aim is to help you build a reading habit with books you can trust.

Delivery availability, charges, and estimated arrival times are confirmed through our existing ordering process.

Simple UPI Payments

We accept payments through UPI.

Customers can follow our existing ordering process to enquire about book availability, confirm their order details, and receive the final payment and delivery information from the seller.

We want the process to remain straightforward and clear, without unnecessary complications.

Our Mission: Less Scrolling, More Reading

We want to contribute to a stronger reading culture in India.

Reading gives us space to think, learn, imagine, question, and grow. A book can introduce us to new ideas, take us into unfamiliar worlds, and help us reconnect with our own thoughts.

Our goal is simple: encourage more people to replace some of their endless screen time with meaningful reading.

Put the scroll on pause. Pick up a book. Rediscover the joy of reading.
